If you use Robi Chat, you already have Pals. Open **Pals** in the sidebar and your pals are right there, with their chats, cards and files.

## Same account, same pals

Robi Chat and Pals share one account. Pals you make in Robi Chat show up in the Pals apps when you sign in with Robi Chat, and the other way round. A card you answer in Robi Chat is answered everywhere.

Your plan carries over too. If you subscribe to Robi Chat, you're on the paid plan in Pals, with unlimited messages.

## What you can do there

From Robi Chat you can talk to your pals, answer their approvals and questions, make new pals, send files, and see their rules, memories and skills. A few things stay in the Pals apps for now: editing routines and triggers, connecting apps, taking over a pal's computer, and saved passwords.

## Built carefully

Robi Chat talks to Pals through the Pals API, as you, with short-lived tokens. It never sees your saved passwords or settings. And you can cut it off at any time from Account in the Pals app: choose Disconnect RobiChat and its access ends immediately.
