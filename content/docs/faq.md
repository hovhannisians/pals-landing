## Do pals keep working when I close the window?

Yes. On the desktop app, closing the window keeps your pals working in the background until you quit with **⌘Q**. On hosted Pals, they keep working with your laptop closed.

## Where does a pal's computer run?

In the cloud, on E2B. Every pal gets its own machine with a real browser. On the desktop app you can also give a pal access to your own computer, and it asks before every command it runs there.

## Can a pal spend my money or send things without asking?

Not unless you've told it that it can. Sending, posting, paying and signing in ask first by default, and you can add **Never** rules for anything you never want a pal to do. See [Rules](/docs/rules).

## Can a pal see my passwords?

No. Saved passwords are typed into the page by Pals itself and never go to the model. See [Saved passwords](/docs/passwords).

## Why did my pal ask me to take over?

It hit something it can't do on its own, like a captcha, a code from your phone, or a payment step. Open the **Computer** tab, choose **Take over**, do the step, and hand it back.

## Why can't my pal click something?

Pals find things on a page by their text. Things drawn on a canvas, like some maps, games and design tools, have no text to find.

## Why didn't my pal read the PDF I sent?

PDFs and other files go to the pal's workspace, not straight to the model. Ask it to open the file, and it will read it with code. Images are sent to the model directly.

## Why didn't my trigger fire straight away?

Most fire within seconds, but triggers with an interval setting, like Gmail's, can take up to that many minutes.

## Can my pal book things for me?

Yes, if the site has a card saved in your account there. Otherwise it takes you to the payment step and asks you to take over. Either way, it asks for your approval before buying.

## Is my data shared between the desktop app and hosted Pals?

No. The desktop app keeps everything on your Mac. Hosted Pals keeps everything in your Robi account. They're separate.

## Where do I find my data on my Mac?

In `~/Library/Application Support/Pals`. See [Your data](/docs/privacy).
