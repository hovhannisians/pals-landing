You can save passwords for the sites your pals use. The pal can then sign in by itself, without ever seeing the password.

## How it works

1. Save a site, a username and a password in **Settings**, under **Passwords**.
2. When a pal needs to sign in, it opens the site's login page and asks to sign in with your saved password. That needs your approval by default.
3. Pals types the username and password straight into the page. The password never goes to the model, and it doesn't appear in the chat.

If there's no saved password for a site, the pal asks you to add one or to take over and log in yourself. A pal will never ask you to paste a password into the chat. If one does, don't.

## How they're stored

- **On your Mac,** passwords are encrypted with a key kept in your macOS Keychain.
- **On hosted Pals,** they're encrypted on the server with a key only the service holds.

You can see which sites have a saved password, but nobody, including you, can read a password back out of Pals. To change one, save it again.

## Two-factor codes

If a site asks for a code from your phone or an authenticator app, the pal asks you to take over for that step. See [The computer](/docs/computer#watching-and-taking-over).
