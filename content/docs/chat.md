Each pal has its own chats. You can start a new chat with a pal any time, and older chats stay in its history.

## Sending a message

Type what you want. You can attach files: images are sent to the model, and PDFs and other files are saved to the pal's [workspace](/docs/files) for it to work with.

While the pal works, the chat shows short activity rows like "Read 4 emails in Gmail" or "Opened united.com", so you can follow along without reading every step.

## Cards

When a pal needs you, it stops and puts a card in the chat. You also get a notification. There are three kinds.

### Needs your approval

The pal wants to do something your rules say it should ask about, like sending an email or clicking **Buy now**. The card says exactly what will happen.

- **Approve** lets it go ahead this once.
- **Deny** stops it. The pal is told you said no and carries on from there.
- **Always allow** approves it and also saves a rule, so it won't ask about that kind of action again. See [Rules](/docs/rules).

### Question for you

The pal needs a decision, like which of three flights you want. It usually offers a few likely answers as buttons, and you can always type your own.

### Connect an app

The pal needs an app you haven't connected yet. The card has a link to sign in. When you're done, the pal picks up where it left off. See [Connected apps](/docs/apps).

## Updates from the pal

Pals message you on their own when they finish something you asked for, when a routine runs, or when a trigger fires. Finished work usually comes with a short title, like "Your research is ready", and files come as attachments you can preview or download.

## Answering on the go

Cards are answered wherever you see them first. Answering a card from a notification, the web or the mobile app answers it everywhere.
