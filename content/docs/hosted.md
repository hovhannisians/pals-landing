Hosted Pals is the same app run as a service. Instead of installing anything, you sign in with your Robi account and your pals live in the cloud.

## Signing in

You sign in with your Robi account. If you use RobiChat, you can also choose **Login with RobiChat**, and RobiChat can talk to your pals on your behalf. Both land in the same Pals account, so your pals and chats are shared between the two.

## What's different from the desktop app

- **A cloud computer per pal.** Every pal gets its own E2B cloud computer, like everywhere else.
- **No keys to manage.** The Robi, E2B and Composio keys belong to the platform. You never see or replace them.
- **Your own apps.** You still sign in to Composio for your own apps, and you get your own Composio user for triggers.
- **Private by account.** Every pal, chat, rule, routine, password and setting belongs to your account. Nobody else can see your data or your live updates.
- **Encrypted secrets.** Saved passwords and tokens are encrypted on the server.

## Plans

Pals doesn't sell anything itself. Your plan comes from the service you signed in with: if you subscribe to RobiChat, you're on the paid plan in Pals too. See [Plans and limits](/docs/plans).

## On your phone

The iPhone and Android apps sign in to the same account. Your pals, chats, rules and cards are the same everywhere, and a card you answer on your phone is answered on the web too.
