A pal is defined by a few things you choose: a name, a job, standing instructions and a look. You can have as many as you like, each with a different job.

## Making a pal

Choose **New pal** in the sidebar. Start from one of the starters or from scratch:

| Starter | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Scout | Research anything you ask and deliver clear, sourced briefs |
| Juniper | Keep an eye on your inbox and draft replies in your voice |
| Atlas | Plan trips and track prices for flights and hotels |
| Bix | Write and run code and scripts on its computer |

The only thing a pal really needs is its job, in plain words. Be specific: "Watch my inbox for anything from my landlord and summarize it" works better than "help with email".

When you create it, the pal greets you in a new chat.

## Identity and instructions

Open a pal's **Setup** tab to change it at any time.

- **Name.** Up to 60 characters.
- **What this pal is for.** Its job. The pal reads this every turn.
- **Instructions.** Tone, preferences, things to always or never do. For example: "Write like me: short, no exclamation marks. Never schedule anything before 10am."
- **Model.** Which Robi model it thinks with. Leave it on the default unless you have a reason.

## Its look

Every pal has a shape, a colour and eye colour. There are five shapes (rounded, diagonal, diamond, octagon and burst) and twelve colours. The planes turning inside each pal start from a random seed, so no two look quite alike.

A pal's face shows what it's doing: it blinks and glances around when it's ready, scans while it's working, looks around when it needs you, and sleeps when it's paused.

## Status

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Ready | Idle and waiting for something to do |
| Working | Doing something. The chat shows what, like "Searching the web" |
| Needs you | Waiting on a card: an approval, a question or an app to connect |
| Paused | Not working and not messaging you until you resume it |

## Stop, pause and delete

- **Stop** ends what the pal is doing right now. It stays ready for the next thing.
- **Pause** stops any ongoing work, and the pal won't message you, run routines or react to triggers until you resume it.
- **Delete** (in **Setup**, under **Danger zone**) removes its chat, memory, routines, triggers and computer. This can't be undone. If the pal leads a channel, the channel is deleted too.
