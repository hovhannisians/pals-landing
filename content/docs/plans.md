Pals has two plans: free, and paid. The paid plan comes with a Robi Chat or Bleenk subscription.

## Where your plan comes from

Pals doesn't sell anything itself. Your plan comes from the service you signed in with. If you subscribe to Robi Chat or Bleenk, you're on the paid plan in Pals too, including during a trial. Everyone else gets the free allowance.

If your plan doesn't look right, sign out and back in. Pals reads your plan again at every sign-in.

## Limits

| | Free | Paid |
|---|---|---|
| Messages a day | 50 | Unlimited |
| Voice minutes a day | 10 | 120 |
| Voice calls at once | 1 | 3 |
| Uploads | 1 GB a day | No daily cap |

Voice limits are the planned limits at launch.

## What uses a message

- Sending a message to a pal or a channel.
- Answering a question card.
- Each routine run, including scheduled runs.
- Each time a trigger fires.
- On a voice call, each piece of real work you ask for, and each question card you answer by voice.

Approving or denying a card is free. So are the pal's own replies, its updates and its hand-offs to other pals.

## When you run out

On the free plan, messages come back at midnight UTC. Until then, sending a message shows how long until they reset and a link to subscribe. Routines and triggers that come due while you're out are skipped, so you won't come back to a pile of runs.

If something fails to start, the message isn't counted.
