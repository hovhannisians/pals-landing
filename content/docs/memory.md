Pals get better the longer you use them, because they keep two kinds of notes: memories about you and skills for tasks they repeat.

## Memory

When a pal learns something worth keeping, it saves it: "Prefers aisle seats." "Dana is their manager." "Weekly report goes out on Fridays." It knows those things in every future chat.

You can tell it directly ("Remember that I'm vegetarian"), and it will forget something if you ask ("Forget my old address"). Each pal has its own memory.

To see or change what a pal remembers, open its **Setup** tab and find **What it knows about you**. You can add, edit and delete memories there.

## Skills

A skill is a set of step-by-step instructions a pal writes for itself after it works out how to do something it will do again, like "How to file an expense report in our company's portal" or "How to pull the weekly numbers from the dashboard".

Next time, the pal loads the skill and follows it, instead of working it out from scratch. You'll find them in **Setup** under **Repeatable know-how**, where you can read, edit or delete them. You can also ask a pal to save a skill: "Save how you just did that as a skill."

## How it all fits together

Before every turn, a pal is reminded of its job, your instructions, your rules, its memories, the names of its skills and its routines. That's why changes you make in **Setup** take effect right away.
