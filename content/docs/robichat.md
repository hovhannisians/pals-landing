Pals is built into Robi Chat. Open **Pals** in the Robi Chat sidebar and your pals are right there, with their chats, cards and files. There's nothing to install.

## Same account, same pals

Robi Chat and Pals share one account. Pals you make in Robi Chat show up in the Pals apps when you choose **Login with RobiChat**, and the other way round. A card you answer in Robi Chat is answered everywhere.

## What you can do in Robi Chat

- Talk to your pals, start new chats, and pause or stop a pal.
- Answer their cards: approvals, questions and requests to connect an app.
- Make new pals and change their name, job and instructions.
- Send files to a pal and download what it sends you.
- See and edit its rules, memories and skills.
- See its routines and triggers.

Some things stay in the Pals apps for now: creating and editing routines and triggers, connecting apps, watching or taking over a pal's computer, saved passwords and settings. Saved passwords and settings never leave the Pals apps.

## Your plan

Your Robi Chat plan carries over. If you subscribe to Robi Chat, you're on the paid plan in Pals too, and upgrading happens in Robi Chat. See [Plans and limits](/docs/plans).

## Disconnecting Robi Chat

You can cut Robi Chat off at any time from **Account** in the Pals app: choose **Disconnect RobiChat**. Robi Chat loses access straight away, and any call or live update it had open ends. To let it back in, choose **Allow RobiChat again** on the same page.

## For developers

Robi Chat talks to Pals through the [Pals API](/docs/api), as you, with short-lived tokens. It never sees your saved passwords or settings.
