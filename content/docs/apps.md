Pals connect to Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, Notion, GitHub and about 1,500 other apps through [Composio](https://composio.dev).

## Connecting apps

In the desktop app, sign in with Composio in **Settings**. The sign-in opens in your normal browser. Then connect the apps you want from **Apps** in the sidebar.

You don't have to connect everything up front. When a pal needs an app you haven't connected, it shows a **Connect an app** card in the chat with a link to sign in. When you're done, it carries on.

## What pals do on their own

Pals read on their own: searching your email, checking your calendar, looking up a Notion page or a GitHub issue.

They ask before they send, post, pay for or change anything: replying to an email, posting in Slack, creating a calendar event, closing an issue. You decide where that line is with [rules](/docs/rules).

## Browsing apps

**Apps** in the sidebar lists every app you can connect, with what a pal can do with each one and a few example requests to try.

## Apps and triggers

[Triggers](/docs/triggers) use apps too, but in the desktop app they run in your own Composio project. You connect the apps for triggers again from a pal's **Setup** page.
