Pals are personal agents that keep working in the background, each on its own computer. You give a pal a job in plain words, like "keep an eye on my inbox and draft replies in my voice", and it gets on with it: it browses, uses your apps, runs code, remembers what it learns about you, and comes back to you when it needs a decision.

Pals think with [Robi](https://robiai.com) models. Pals is a Robi Labs product.

## What a pal has

- **Its own computer.** A cloud computer powered by E2B, with a real browser and a workspace for files and code.
- **Its own browser.** A Chrome profile that stays logged in to the sites you sign it in to. You can watch it and take over at any point.
- **Your apps.** Gmail, Calendar, Slack, Notion, GitHub and about 1,500 more, connected through Composio.
- **Your rules.** Plain-language rules about what it may do on its own, what it must ask about, and what it must never do.
- **A memory.** Facts about you and how you like things done, and skills for tasks it repeats.

## How you work with it

You talk to a pal in a chat. Most of the time it just reports back. When it wants to do something that matters, such as sending an email, posting, paying or changing something, it stops and shows you a card. You approve it, deny it, or tell it to stop asking for that kind of thing.

You can also put a pal on a schedule, wake it up when something happens in your apps, and make several pals with different jobs that pass work to each other.

## Two ways to run it

Pals runs from the same code in two ways. The Mac and Windows apps can run your pals on your own computer, and the iPhone and Android apps connect to your hosted pals.

| | On your computer | Hosted |
|---|---|---|
| Account | None. The app runs its own local server | Sign in with your Robi account |
| Computers | A cloud computer per pal, on E2B | A cloud computer per pal, on E2B |
| Keys | You paste your Robi key | Provided by the platform |
| Data | On your computer, like `~/Library/Application Support/Pals` on a Mac | Your account, on Robi's servers |
| Limits | None | 50 messages a day on the free plan |
