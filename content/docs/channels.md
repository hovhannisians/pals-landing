You can make a few pals with different jobs and have them work together, the way a small team would.

## Pals asking each other

Any pal can message another of your pals to ask for help or hand off part of a task. Juniper might ask Scout to research the three restaurants before drafting the invitation, or Atlas might ask Bix to write a script that compares prices.

You see the hand-off in the chat. A pal can pass work on, and that pal can pass it on once more, but no further, so work doesn't bounce around forever. A paused pal can't be asked.

## Channels

A channel is a group chat with several pals at once.

1. Choose **New channel** in the sidebar.
2. Pick at least two pals and give the channel a name.
3. Choose one pal to lead with **Make lead**.

When you post in a channel, the lead decides who should do what and delegates to the other members. Their replies show up in the channel, so you can see the whole conversation.

Channels are good for work that crosses jobs: planning a trip (travel, calendar and budget), launching something (research, writing and code), or running a household (inbox, bills and errands).

## Good to know

- A channel can have up to 20 pals.
- Deleting a pal removes it from its channels. If it led a channel, the channel is deleted.
- On the hosted free plan, a post in a channel uses one message, like a message to a single pal.
