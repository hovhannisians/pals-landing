You can call a pal and talk to it out loud. It answers in its own voice, and it can still do real work while you talk: "Find me three flights to Lisbon for Friday" starts the same work a typed message would, and the pal tells you when it's done.

> **Coming soon.** Voice calls are on the way to hosted Pals and the phone apps.

## During a call

- What you say and what the pal says are both saved to the chat, so you can read the call back later.
- When you ask for real work, the pal starts it and keeps talking with you. If it takes a while, it tells you it has started and reports back when it finishes.
- The pal mentions when a card needs you, when a deliverable is ready, and when it has put a file in the chat.
- You can tell it to remember something, and it saves the memory right away.
- If you talk over the pal, it stops and listens.

## Approvals by voice

Cards still need your answer:

- A question can be answered by voice.
- An approval can be given by voice only after the pal reads back exactly what will happen and you say yes.
- **Always allow** is never offered by voice. Make rules in the app.

## Voices

Each pal can have its own voice: Alloy, Ash, Ballad, Coral, Cedar, Echo, Marin, Sage, Shimmer or Verse. The default is Marin.

## Limits

These are the planned limits at launch and may change.

| | Free | Paid |
|---|---|---|
| Minutes a day | 10 | 120 |
| Calls at once | 1 | 3 |
| Longest call | 30 minutes | 30 minutes |

The call itself doesn't use messages. Asking the pal to start real work, or answering a question card by voice, uses one message, the same as typing it would.
